About

Hey there, I'm Adam.

I'm a UX designer and eCommerce experience strategist in Ohio. I help online shoppers get where they're going with less friction and more confidence, and I help businesses see the results.

For the past few years I've owned UX across four eCommerce brands at National Vision: AmericasBest, Eyeglass World, Military Optical, and Discount Contacts. I work on the whole customer journey, from navigation and product pages to checkout, and I use research, analytics, and A/B testing to make sure design changes actually work.

Before that, I spent years in freelance design and print, which taught me to deliver quality work on a deadline and listen closely to what people need.

What I believe about UX

Good UX improves lives. It breaks down barriers and leads people toward three things:

Progress
It helps people reach their goals, whether that's finding the right product or finishing checkout without a second thought.
Peace
It protects people from errors and uses honest, plain language, so they feel safe.
Joy
It makes people feel capable and smart because the design is easy to understand and enjoyable to use.
